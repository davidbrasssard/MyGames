Tile depth refactor (no visual change intended). Not committed.
Read first: Mahjong (kit/Tile.tsx+css, mahjong/geometry.ts) and Trio (tripletile/{geometry,tileLook}.ts, tripletile.css) had the same recipe in two copies: same stepped left+bottom thickness, rim, layer lift/lean = thickness, per-layer darkening. They differed in numbers (Mahjong thickness 0.11 both axes; Trio 0.06 left / 0.115 bottom), palette, face (gradient vs flat), border (2.2% vs 2px), shadow (Mahjong blurred box-shadow; Trio separate unblurred shape), colour steps (4 side tones vs 1).
Moved to NEW kit/tileDepth.ts + kit/tileDepth.css: tileDepth() geometry (faceW, row, lift, lean), paletteVars() shading, BLOCKED_GREY, flatSides(); CSS classes .td-solid (sides+rim), .td-blocked (grey layer), .td-drop (ground shadow, no blur).
Games now supply only palette + rules: kit/Tile.tsx (Mahjong palette), tripletile/tileLook.ts (Trio palette). mahjong/geometry.ts and tripletile/geometry.ts call tileDepth() (old exports kept). Home icons and the /dev pages go through these, so they use the kit too.

Differences kept on purpose (to avoid visual change), against your recipe:
 - Mahjong's ground shadow is still a blurred box-shadow (kit .td-drop is no-blur, used by Trio only). Switching Mahjong needs a wrapper around its tile = small look change.
 - Mahjong's free faces still darken per layer; Trio's stay fixed (palette flag: fixed vs shaded). Moving Mahjong to fixed faces changes its look.
 - Thickness/face ratios are still per game (passed to tileDepth), not one global ratio.
